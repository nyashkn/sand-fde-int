# Lane: Systems — Rwanda MoH Digital-Health Landscape (MECE Inventory + Deep Dives)

*Research date: 2026-08-09. All claims carry inline source URLs. Where public documentation is thin (MoH-internal systems), confidence is flagged explicitly — nothing is invented.*

---

## Overview

Rwanda's MoH digital-health estate is a layered, standards-based national architecture rather than a single system: a **DHIS2-based aggregate reporting backbone (RHMIS)**, **OpenMRS-derived facility and community EMRs (eBuzima / cEMR)**, a **logistics stack around eLMIS and Rwanda Medical Supply (RMS)**, a **laboratory layer (LIMS/VLSM)**, and a **national interoperability bus (RHIE/OpenHIM)** with national registries (client, facility, provider, terminology, lab, shared health records) that links them [rhie](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf). In April 2025 the MoH launched the **Health Intelligence Center (HIC)** — a six-layer data lakehouse that ingests DHIS2, EMRs, CRVS, eLMIS, WelTel, EMS, workforce and survey data for real-time analytics and AI [moh-nhic](https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions). Ownership is concentrated in the **MoH / Rwanda Biomedical Centre (RBC)** for health systems, with **RISA/MINICT** for national digital infrastructure and **NCSA** for cybersecurity/data-protection compliance [rhie](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf). External partners (Global Fund, CDC/PEPFAR, USAID, WHO, HISP, Gates Foundation, Mastercard/Microsoft) fund and co-develop specific layers, with the Global Fund's National Strategy Financing model and Digital Health Impact Accelerator (DHIA) currently driving the EMR/HIE/portal scale-up [gf](https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf). The strategy layer is in flux: the National Digital Health Strategic Plan 2018–2023 is being updated and a Digital Health Architecture Blueprint is under development, both planned for completion in 2026 [gf](https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf).

## 1. Discovered inventory (MECE)

### A. Routine reporting & aggregate surveillance (national backbone)

- **HMIS / RHMIS on DHIS2** — National routine health reporting platform; every facility submits monthly service-utilization, surveillance, commodity and program data; used by MoH/RBC for national-to-facility performance tracking. DHIS2 adopted 2012; most routine systems (TracNet, SISCom, eIDSR, health financing, eTB) migrated onto it. Source: [RHIE report p.21-22](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf), [OpenHealthNews DHIS2 feature](https://www.openhealthnews.com/articles/2017/dhis2-transforming-health-it-standards-developing-world-part-2), [pplx-inventory](out/.raw/pplx_systems_inventory.json)
- **DHIS2 Tracker — HIV Case-Based Surveillance (CBS)** — Individual-level HIV case tracking (new infections, linkage, viral suppression); deployed in all 197 PEPFAR sites since 2018; OpenMRS now the primary CBS source pushing data to the DHIS2 CBS tracker via OpenHIM mediators. Source: [RHIE report §6.3.1.7](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **Integrated Disease Surveillance & Response (eIDSR)** — Outbreak/epidemic surveillance, migrated onto DHIS2/RHMIS; digitized surveillance (incl. eCHIS linkage to HMIS) expanded real-time pandemic-ready surveillance (malaria hotspots, TB case finding, HIV suppression) — Source: [GF case study p.3](https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf), [RHIE report p.21](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **Cancer / NCD / hepatitis / TB registries** — DHIS2 tailored to monitor eTB records, cancer registries, NCD tracking, hepatitis monitoring, fortified-food management for vulnerable groups. Source: [RHIE report p.21-22](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)

### B. Facility & community clinical systems (EMR)

- **OpenMRS / eBuzima (facility EMR)** — MoH's principal EMR since 2012 (HIV patient management; hospital package adds registration, IPD/OPD consultations, billing, lab, pharmacy); upgraded 1.9.11→2.5.6, moving to OpenMRS 3.x (O3); ~60% of health facilities as of the 2026 GF case study; 42 hospitals + 450+ health centers per RHIE report. Source: [GF case study p.4](https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf), [RHIE report §6.3.1.8](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf), [Rwanda-EMR GitHub org](https://github.com/Rwanda-EMR)
- **cEMR (community EMR)** — Community-level EMR used by 20,000+ CHWs via tablets/smartphones; feeds the future Health Portal and continuity of care between community and facility. Source: [GF case study p.4](https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf)
- **OpenClinic (alternative facility EMR)** — Some hospitals opted for OpenClinic instead of OpenMRS. Source: [RHIE report p.21-22](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **Health Portal (patient-facing)** — National portal under development (DHIA-funded, ~US$2M) drawing on eBuzima/cEMR data so patients can access their own records; part of patient-empowerment agenda. Source: [GF news release](https://www.theglobalfund.org/en/updates/2024/2024-07-03-rwanda-global-fund-new-grants-aids-tb-malaria-strengthen-health-systems/), [GF case study p.4](https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf)

### C. Community health & mHealth

- **eCHIS (electronic Community Health Information System)** — Digitized CHW platform linked to national HMIS; Global Fund-financed; used for surveillance at community level (malaria, TB, HIV) and community-service digitalization. Source: [GF case study p.3-4](https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf)
- **RapidSMS (legacy mHealth)** — SMS-based pregnancy/newborn monitoring and emergency "Red Alert" reporting; launched Musanze 2009, nationwide 2012; still referenced as foundational, being superseded by newer CHW information systems. Source: [RHIE report p.21-22](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf), [pplx-inventory](out/.raw/pplx_systems_inventory.json)
- **CHIS — Community & Health Post Information System** — New digitization of community and health-post services, piloted in Kigali and select districts, rolling out countrywide. Source: [RHIE report p.22](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **WelTel** — Population-health tracking (SMS/mobile patient-engagement platform) listed among HIC source systems. Source: [MoH HIC announcement](https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions)

### D. Supply chain / logistics

- **eLMIS (Electronic Logistics Management Information System)** — National commodity ordering, consumption capture, distribution visibility and inventory for all public health facilities and district pharmacies; launched 2014; supports RMS procurement/storage/distribution; cloud-based; interoperates with DHIS2 (HMIS) and the RMS warehouse software. Source: [RHIE report p.22](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf), [pplx-elmis](out/.raw/pplx_elmis_hic.json)
- **RMS automated warehouse / WMS** — Warehouse-execution software at Rwanda Medical Supply central medical store, integrated with eLMIS for automated order processing and inventory-status sharing. Source: [pplx-elmis](out/.raw/pplx_elmis_hic.json)
- **Reagent Management System™ (ERP)** — Enterprise-resource-planning system for lab reagents; MoH prioritizes complete interoperability between lab information systems, HMIS and this system for end-to-end visibility across testing, results and stock. Source: [GF case study p.3](https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf)

### E. Laboratory

- **National Laboratory Information System (LIMS) / web-based LMIS** — Viral load and early infant diagnosis (EID) lab data management, maintained with Global Fund financing; user training and capacity at the National Reference Laboratory; reduced turnaround times and improved HMIS data completeness. Source: [GF case study p.3](https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf)
- **VLSM (Viral Load System Management) + Labware** — External lab systems integrated with EMRs via the RHIE lab registry (sample orders/results, JSON and FHIR); EMR↔VLSM exchange implemented over OpenHIM. Source: [RHIE report §6.3.1.6](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **National Reference Laboratory (NRL)** — Reference testing hub whose capacity, training and systems are partly Global Fund-financed. Source: [GF case study p.3](https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf)

### F. Identity, registries & interoperability (RHIE)

- **RHIE — Rwanda Health Information Exchange** — OpenHIE-based national interoperability platform; minimum viable product connected first Kigali sites by 2023 for HIV CBS and indicator reporting; now hosted at the national data center. Source: [RHIE report exec summary](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **OpenHIM (mediator)** — Middleware routing/translating transactions between EMRs and national registries; upgraded 5.12→8.2 with Grafana/Prometheus monitoring; supports JSON and FHIR EMR↔VLSM flows via HAPI FHIR. Source: [RHIE report §6.3.1.1](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf), [openhim.org](https://openhim.org/)
- **Client Registry + Unique Patient ID (UPID)** — National patient identity; integrated with NIDA (National Identification Agency) to auto-fill demographics (~90% of registration fields); issues cross-facility UPID (YYMMDD-FacilityCode-Random). Source: [RHIE report §6.3.1.2](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **Facility Registry (FOSA IDs)** — National master facility repository, unique facility codes; deployed in testing environment, to be managed by MoH admins. Source: [RHIE report §6.3.1.3](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **Provider Registry** — Health-worker registry integrated with professional councils; license validity gates EMR access; testing environment. Source: [RHIE report §6.3.1.3](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **Shared Health Records (SHR)** — Centralized FHIR patient record (encounters, observations, service requests, medication requests); deployed in production, under-utilized pending clinician training and consent/privacy work. Source: [RHIE report §6.3.1.4](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **Terminology Registry** — ICD-11, ICHI, LOINC, National Product Catalog (NPC) mappings; tested, awaiting operationalization. Source: [RHIE report §6.3.1.5](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **Laboratory Registry** — RHIE service bridging EMRs to VLSM/Labware for HIV lab tests (viral load, CD4, recency); MySQL-backed; testing environment. Source: [RHIE report §6.3.1.6](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **CRVS (Civil Registration & Vital Statistics)** — Birth/death registration layer; listed as HIC source system; linked to health identity and insurance enrollment. Source: [MoH HIC announcement](https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions), [pplx-inventory](out/.raw/pplx_systems_inventory.json)

### G. Workforce, financing, admin & support

- **CSAM — Clinical Staff Application Management System** — Web-based recruitment/transfer of health professionals (launched 2023). Source: [RHIE report p.22](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **HWMS — Health Workforce Management System** — Workforce management; HIC source system. Source: [MoH HIC announcement](https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions)
- **Health Facilities Licensing Platform** — Digitized licensing, account creation to e-license issuance. Source: [RHIE report p.22](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **MEMMS — Medical Equipment Management & Maintenance System** — Equipment registration, routine/emergency maintenance, provider-maintainer communication (mobile app added). Source: [RHIE report p.22](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **HRTT — Health Resource Tracking Tool** — Health-sector financial flows tracking (since 2013), being updated. Source: [RHIE report p.22](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **Blood Bank Information System** — National blood management, revamped for safety and donor engagement; in use. Source: [RHIE report p.22](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **eLearning** — Health-sector e-learning platform (since Jan 2018). Source: [RHIE report p.22](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **Rwanda Health Analytics Platform** — National analytics/BI platform; capacity building ongoing at national and subnational levels. Source: [GF case study p.4](https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf)

### H. Intelligence / analytics

- **National Health Intelligence Center (HIC)** — Launched 3 April 2025 at the Global AI Summit on Africa; six-layer architecture (Source → Ingestion/Transformation → Replication/Landing Zone → Data Lakehouse → Storage → Presentation); ingests DHIS2, eBuzima, cEMR, CRVS, eLMIS, WelTel, EMS, HWMS, census/surveys (e.g., DHS). Source: [MoH news](https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions), [NHIC site](https://nhic.moh.gov.rw/), [furtherAfrica](https://furtherafrica.com/2025/04/24/rwanda-launches-health-intelligence-centre-to-predict-disease-outbreaks/)

## 2. Double-click: the 5 most central systems

### 2.1 HMIS / DHIS2 (RHMIS) — the aggregate backbone

- **Purpose**: national routine health reporting, service utilization, disease surveillance, commodity reporting, performance monitoring at national/district/facility level; also hosts program registries (eTB, cancer, NCD, hepatitis, fortified foods) and the HIV CBS Tracker. Source: [RHIE report p.21-22](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf), [OpenHealthNews](https://www.openhealthnews.com/articles/2017/dhis2-transforming-health-it-standards-developing-world-part-2)
- **Owner**: MoH with RBC as implementing agency; HISP Rwanda provides technical capacity. Source: [RHIE report](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf), [HISP Rwanda](https://hisprwanda.org/beyond-fragmentation-hisp-rwanda-advances-strategic-emr-dhis2-integration-at-global-fund-architecture-workshop)
- **Architecture**: open-source DHIS2 (Web-based, cloud-hosted); adopted 2012 after legacy systems failed to share data across facilities; supports both aggregate datasets and tracker (individual) programs; near-real-time national tracking; a key HIC source layer. Source: [OpenHealthNews](https://www.openhealthnews.com/articles/2017/dhis2-transforming-health-it-standards-developing-world-part-2), [MoH HIC announcement](https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions)
- **Users**: all health facilities (monthly reporting), district/national M&E officers, disease programs, HIC data pipelines. Source: [OpenHealthNews](https://www.openhealthnews.com/articles/2017/dhis2-transforming-health-it-standards-developing-world-part-2)

### 2.2 OpenMRS / eBuzima + cEMR — clinical records at facility & community

- **Purpose**: longitudinal patient-level clinical records — HIV care (core), plus registration, IPD/OPD consultations, lab orders, pharmacy, billing in the hospital package; cEMR extends this to community level. Source: [RHIE report §6.3.1.8](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf), [GF case study p.4](https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf)
- **Owner**: MoH/RBC; developed with CDC/PEPFAR, ICAP, UCSF, QT Global Software (local vendor engaged via RISA outsourcing), Jembi, PIH; code public on GitHub (Rwanda-EMR org: mohbilling, rwandareports, laboratorymanagement-v2, pharmacymanagement, mohappointment, mohtracportal, O3 ESM). Source: [RHIE report §5.3.1](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf), [github.com/Rwanda-EMR](https://github.com/Rwanda-EMR)
- **Architecture / modules**: OpenMRS 1.9.11 → 2.5.6 (core), Tomcat 8.5/9, Ubuntu 20.04 LTS, MambaETL for heavy reports; CBS forms (enrolment/follow-up, index testing, partner notification, recency), MER indicators, auto-billing; upgrade path to OpenMRS 3.x (O3) UI in progress (4th hackathon, Aug 2024). Source: [RHIE report §6.3.1.8](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **Scale**: 42 hospitals + 450+ health centers (RHIE report); eBuzima in ~60% of health facilities (GF 2026); cEMR used by 20,000+ CHWs. Source: [RHIE report](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf), [GF case study p.4](https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf)
- **Known gaps**: uneven adoption; services (surgery, maternity, anesthesia, FP, pediatrics, ophthalmology) still paper-based in hospital package; CBS inconsistent with DHIS2 at some sites; no insurance-system integration; SHR under-utilized. Source: [RHIE report §7.3](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)

### 2.3 RHIE — interoperability bus & national registries

- **Purpose**: connect siloed systems via OpenHIE patterns: OpenHIM mediator + client/facility/provider/terminology/lab registries + Shared Health Records; MVP focused on HIV CBS and indicator reporting (first Kigali sites 2023). Source: [RHIE report exec summary](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **Owner**: MoH (Digitization Directorate/CDO), built by QT Global Software; hosted at national data center; CDC/PEPFAR funded the foundational phase; U.S. CDC TSSS Strategic Information component. Source: [RHIE report §5.2, §6.2.2, §8.1.1](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **Data flows (documented lab flow)**: EMR submits lab order (JSON) → OpenHIM routes to Mapping Mediator + Lab Registry → mediator converts to FHIR → stores in HAPI FHIR → confirmation back to EMR; VLSM result flow mirrored; EMR retrieves results via FHIR query. Source: [RHIE report §5.3.2 flow diagram](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)
- **Status**: Client Registry (UPID, NIDA-integrated) + SHR live at PEPFAR sites (Kibagabaga: 462,865 encounters/7 months); Facility/Provider/Terminology/Lab registries in testing; clinician training and consent frameworks pending; standards docs ~3 years old, being updated. Source: [RHIE report §6.3.2, §7](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf)

### 2.4 eLMIS / RMS supply chain + lab interoperability

- **Purpose**: end-to-end commodity visibility — ordering, consumption, distribution, inventory — for all public facilities and district pharmacies; RMS procurement/storage/distribution; automated warehouse execution at central store; Reagent Management System (ERP) bridges lab stock. Source: [RHIE report p.22](https://ciichin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rwanda-Health-Information-System-Ecosystem-RHIE-Achievement-over-last-Three-years-Full-Report.pdf), [GF case study p.3](https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf), [pplx-elmis](out/.raw/pplx_elmis_hic.json)
- **Owner**: MoH/RBC with RMS operating the supply chain; USAID/GHSC-PSM and Global Fund implementation support. Source: [pplx-elmis](out/.raw/pplx_elmis_hic.json)
- **Architecture**: cloud-based eLMIS (launched 2014), integrated with DHIS2/HMIS and RMS WMS; priority MoH goal = complete interoperability of LIMS + HMIS + Reagent Management System. Source: [pplx-elmis](out/.raw/pplx_elmis_hic.json), [GF case study p.3](https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf)
- **Users**: health facility staff, district pharmacies, RMS central warehouse, MoH/RBC supply-chain decision-makers; eLMIS also feeds HIC. Source: [pplx-elmis](out/.raw/pplx_elmis_hic.json), [MoH HIC announcement](https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions)

### 2.5 National Health Intelligence Center (HIC)

- **Purpose**: real-time national health analytics hub — collect, integrate, triangulate, analyze data across the health pyramid; AI-driven surveillance, resource allocation, policy support, early warning (e.g., outbreak prediction). Source: [MoH news](https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions), [NHIC](https://nhic.moh.gov.rw/), [furtherAfrica](https://furtherafrica.com/2025/04/24/rwanda-launches-health-intelligence-centre-to-predict-disease-outbreaks/)
- **Owner**: Ministry of Health (flagship initiative, launched 3 Apr 2025, Global AI Summit on Africa, Kigali). Source: [MoH news](https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions)
- **Architecture (six layers)**: 1) Source — DHIS2/HMIS, EMRs (eBuzima, cEMR), CRVS, eLMIS, WelTel, Emergency Medical Services, HWMS, census/surveys (DHS); 2) Data Ingestion & Transformation (standardize/clean); 3) Data Replication (landing zone, raw); 4) Data Lakehouse (validate, enrich, aggregate); 5) Data Storage (secure, scalable); 6) Presentation (dashboards, reports, APIs for external stakeholders). Source: [MoH news](https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions)
- **Users**: MoH leadership and policymakers, disease programs, subnational managers (via Rwanda Health Analytics Platform), partner stakeholders via APIs. Source: [MoH news](https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions), [GF case study p.4](https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf)
