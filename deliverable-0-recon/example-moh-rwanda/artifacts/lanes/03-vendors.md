# Vendors + AI — who supplies the Rwanda MoH stack

**Lane:** vendors + AI (L3) · **Method:** Perplexity sonar-pro (×10) + Parallel.ai search (×1) + native fetches (HISP Rwanda, Viebeg, GeekWire, WEF, OpenAI, AfDB) · Raw dumps: `out/.raw/pplx_*.json`, `out/.raw/parallel_vendors_discover.json`, `out/.raw/pplx_viebeg_afdb.json`

## Overview

Rwanda's MoH digital-health stack follows a **ministry-owned, partner-implemented model**: the MoH/RBC own and govern the national platforms (DHIS2/HMIS, OpenMRS EMRs, eLMIS, RapidSMS/RapidPro, NHIC), while **HISP Rwanda** is the anchor implementer for the DHIS2 ecosystem and **PIH/Inshuti Mu Buzima** historically anchored OpenMRS deployments. The AI layer is where the supplier landscape is most dynamic and least consolidated: **Babyl** ran the flagship AI-triage/telemedicine service until Babylon wound it down (Aug 2023); **Zipline** and **Viebeg** supply AI-enabled logistics/forecasting; **Insightiv** and **Digital Umuganda** are the leading local AI startups (imaging AI + Kinyarwanda LLMs); and 2025–26 brought platform-level agreements with **Anthropic** (MOU with MINICT) and **OpenAI/Gates Foundation** (Horizon1000, piloting in Rwanda) plus a reported **RBC–OpenEvidence** decision-support partnership. Public contract/grant detail is thin — most engagements are framed as partnerships, grants, or PPP-supported service arrangements rather than published tenders.

## Double-click: the central AI vendors and the anchor implementer

### 1. HISP Rwanda — the anchor implementer (non-AI, context for the stack)

Private Kigali company positioning itself as "your digitalization partner"; deploys and customises DHIS2-based HIS across 11 countries (Rwanda, CAR, Comoros, Djibouti, Sudan, Burundi, Congo-Brazzaville, Chad, Madagascar, Gabon, Jamaica). Its partner wall names the Republic of Rwanda MoH, **RBC**, RISA, USAID, UNICEF, WHO, Gavi, Global Fund, University of Oslo, and MSH — i.e., it is the shared implementer across the MoH/RBC/donor ecosystem, not just a software vendor. At the DHIS2 Annual Conference 2025 it showcased a **DHIS2–mSupply integrated eLMIS** (built for Comoros with HISP Centre/UiO and the mSupply Foundation), the same stack family used for Rwanda logistics. Contract terms are not public; the engagement model is PPP/partner-funded. Sources: [hisprwanda.org](https://hisprwanda.org/), [eLMIS blog post](https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-rwanda-showcases-innovative-dhis2msupply-elmis-solution-at-dhis2-annual-conference-2025)

### 2. Babyl (Babylon Health) — AI triage (status: wound down)

- **Product:** AI-powered symptom triage + telemedicine (consultation, prescription, referral, lab ordering) over SMS/voice, usable on basic phones.
- **Integration point:** front-door triage in Rwanda's digital health system; consultations paid via **Mutuelle de Santé** (RSSB-managed national insurance); part of the 10-year "digital-first integrated care" partnership with the Government of Rwanda (2020); access for every person over 12; Gates Foundation co-funding reported.
- **Data used:** registration data, phone number/national ID, symptom data entered by patients; a spokesperson said government-shared data was **non-personal statistical data**; scale: 2.6M+ registered patients and ~4,000 consultations/day (late 2021); ~2.8M users (~20% of population) by end-2022.
- **Status:** Babylon **wound down Babyl Rwanda in Aug 2023** (company financial distress); no public evidence of an ongoing successor operation — for 2024–26 treat as legacy/transitioned. Sources: [MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/emea/babylon-inks-10-year-partnership-rwandan-government), [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20211203005293/en/Babylon-Launches-AI-in-Rwanda-in-Next-Step-Towards-Digitising-Healthcare-in-Rwanda), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/katiejennings/2023/08/11/babylon-winding-down-operations-in-rwanda-potentially-disrupting-care-for-28-million-people/), [TelecareAware](https://telecareaware.com/babylon-health-files-for-us-chapter-7-bankruptcy-winding-down-babyl-rwanda-and-ending-care-for-2-8-million-users/), [Healthcare Digital](https://healthcare-digital.com/digital-healthcare/babylon-health-digitising-rwandas-healthcare-system)

### 3. Zipline — AI logistics (operational)

- **Product:** autonomous fixed-wing drone delivery network; the "AI" is in **flight-path optimisation, scheduling, weather-aware rerouting, airspace management** (cloud planner updating waypoints every 30s, reinforcement-learning path-finding).
- **Integration point:** delivery layer of the supply chain — blood and emergency commodities from central storage, vaccines to health posts via **RBC partnership** (real-time demand/inventory "pull" model); reported integration of delivery/logistics data with national health and emergency-response systems.
- **Data used:** delivery orders, inventory/demand signals, weather/airspace data; **eLMIS linkage not explicitly confirmed** in public sources.
- **Contract context:** paid by GoR health authorities per delivery (2016 origin), expanded to nationwide service with the Government of Rwanda. Sources: [Zipline newsroom](https://www.zipline.com/newsroom/zipline-and-the-government-of-rwanda-announce-a-new-partnership-to-serve-the-entire-country-with-instant-logistics), [Zipline × RBC vaccines](https://www.zipline.com/newsroom/bringing-vaccines-closer-to-home-a-partnership-to-expand-access-in-rwanda), [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/technology-37646474), [PC Tech Mag](https://pctechmag.com/2025/07/rwandas-smart-drones-and-ai-cut-medical-delivery-time/), [ITU](https://www.itu.int/hub/2020/04/how-medical-delivery-drones-are-improving-lives-in-rwanda/)

### 4. Viebeg Technologies — AI supply chain/forecasting

- **Product (per its own site):** "integrated health intelligence platform" = **AI-powered diagnostics, disease-burden forecasting, Smart Clinic deployment, predictive maintenance**, plus procurement optimisation (VieProcure marketplace) and asset-lifecycle management.
- **Integration point:** medical-commodities procurement/inventory layer for facilities (order anywhere/anytime via online store; equipment + consumables catalogues); Rwanda customers include Rwanda Military Hospital, King Faisal Hospital, Nemba DH and multiple clinics.
- **Data used:** facility procurement orders, inventory levels, supply/demand signals, equipment asset data (from product descriptions); no published schema.
- **Contract context:** commercial B2B (1,000+ customers), not a published MoH tender. Funding link to AfDB **confirmed (indirect)**: the **Rwanda Innovation Fund** — partly financed by the **African Development Bank** (AfDB states a **$30M** investment in the fund) — invested in Viebeg's data-driven logistics platform (Viebeg's ~$1M round reported in 2021 by secondary coverage); CEO Tobias Reiter frames it as an AI-driven medical-procurement platform connecting providers to manufacturers. This is indirect fund/equity support, **not a direct AfDB grant or loan to Viebeg**. Sources: [viebeg.com](https://www.viebeg.com), [AfDB success story](https://www.afdb.org/en/success-stories/how-rwanda-using-artificial-intelligence-improve-healthcare-55309), [AfDB MapAfrica project (Rwanda Innovation Fund)](https://mapafrica.afdb.org/en/projects/46002-P-RW-G00-001), [HealthTimes Africa (RIF invests in Viebeg)](https://www.healthtimesafrica.com/article/555877707-ensuring-millions-get-proper-care-the-rwanda-innovation-fund-rif-invests-in-rwanda-based-viebeg-technologies)

### 5. Insightiv — local imaging AI + CHW tools

- **Product:** AI-assisted teleradiology platform (workflow, anomaly localisation/detection, reporting, PACS/imaging-viewer interoperability; CT/MRI/X-ray); COVID-19 CXR AI (reported ~94% accuracy); C-section wound-infection AI for CHW home visits; contributed to the **mUbuzima** CHW app with IMB/Harvard/MIT.
- **Integration point:** hospital diagnostics layer (public + private hospitals; pilots with Rwanda FDA and MoH oversight); community layer via CHW maternal-health workflows.
- **Data used:** medical images (chest X-rays etc.), ~1,000+ wound images from rural Rwanda for training, case/workflow metadata; no EHR-text/claims data documented.
- **Contract context:** MIT IDEAS 2020 grant ($16k); no confirmed VC round; regulatory approval in process at last public reporting. Sources: [New Times](https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/194633/News/rwandan-scientist-wants-to-use-ai-to-deepen-radiology-services), [RwandaToday](https://rwandatoday.africa/rwanda/news/ai-start-ups-step-into-health-sector-to-simplify-deliver-solutions-and-information-3992468), [Devex](https://www.devex.com/news/sponsored/q-a-rwanda-s-radiology-problem-gets-a-startup-solution-101507), [thehealthtech.org](https://thehealthtech.org/insightiv-a-rwandan-based-radiology-tech-start-up/), [PMC12838494 (mUbuzima)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12838494/)

### 6. Anthropic — platform-level public-sector AI

- **Product/agreement:** 3-year MOU with the Government of Rwanda (signed with **MINICT**, Minister Paula Ingabire) to embed Claude across **health, education and public-sector systems**; government developers get Claude + Claude Code, hands-on training and API credits.
- **Integration point (health):** not a named clinical product yet — framed as supporting MoH national priorities (cervical-cancer elimination, malaria, maternal-mortality reduction) and building national AI capacity; Tech Policy Press notes the accountability/oversight gap of this becoming "the backbone of Rwanda's government."
- **Data used:** not specified publicly; the MOU precedes any disclosed health-data pipeline. ### 7. Gates Foundation × OpenAI — Horizon1000 (piloting in Rwanda)

- **Product:** AI tools for primary-care workers — patient intake, triage, follow-up, referrals, and trusted medical information **in local languages**; "augment, not replace" health workers; aligned with national clinical guidelines; optimised for privacy/security per the announcement.
- **Integration point:** frontline primary-care clinics (Rwanda first; 1,000 clinics across Africa by 2028), i.e., the facility/community tier alongside eCHIS/cEMR-type workflows.
- **Data used:** not disclosed in detail; Gates Notes frames it as extending clinical capacity in workforce-shortage settings; OpenAI also launched **ChatGPT Health** (Jan 2026) as its healthcare product family.
- **Contract context:** up to **$50M** combined funding/tech/technical support from the two organisations; announced at WEF 2026 with Rwanda's ICT minister on stage. Sources: [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/gates-foundation-openai-launch-50m-ai-health-initiative-targeting-1000-clinics-in-africa/), [OpenAI Horizon1000](https://openai.com/index/horizon-1000/), [Gates Notes](https://www.gatesnotes.com/expanding-access-to-health-care-through-ai), [AP/KSAT](https://www.ksat.com/health/2026/01/22/rwanda-to-test-ai-powered-technology-in-clinics-under-a-new-gates-foundation-project/)

### 8. Digital Umuganda — Kinyarwanda LLMs for diagnosis

- **Product:** locally trained **Kinyarwanda language models** used to assist disease diagnosis (WEF: "in partnership with Digital Umuganda, locally trained language models in Kinyarwanda assist in diagnosing diseases"); CEO Audace Niyonkuru told AP the Kinyarwanda-first design removes language barriers to care.
- **Integration point:** clinical diagnosis/decision-support layer; exact EMR/health-system integration not documented; **no direct MoH/RBC contract found**.
- **Data used:** Kinyarwanda-language clinical interaction data (no schema published). Sources: [WEF Rwanda AI-health video/article](https://www.weforum.org/stories/emerging-technologies/data-access-to-healthcare-in-rwanda/) (per extracts), [AP via KSAT](https://www.ksat.com/health/2026/01/22/rwanda-to-test-ai-powered-technology-in-clinics-under-a-new-gates-foundation-project/)

### 9. CHW decision support — d-IDS in the national cEMR (state-backed, vendor unnamed)

- **Product:** **digital integrated disease screening (d-IDS)** embedded in the national community EMR (cEMR) used on CHW smartphones — guides case registration, symptom assessment, diagnostic testing, and treatment/referral decisions; a Rwanda implementation study reports **24.2% fewer referrals**.
- **Integration point:** the national CHW platform (cEMR/eCHIS family) run by MoH/RBC with USAID Tubeho (Jhpiego/SFH/FIND) support — i.e., this is the *institutional* CHW decision-support AI, distinct from Insightiv's research apps.
- **Data used:** CHW-entered patient assessment data at household level.
- **Caveat:** the public abstract does not name the developer; full text was not retrievable (PubMed reCAPTCHA). Source: [PubMed 41687454](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41687454/), [MoH/RBC Digitization Directorate deck](https://www.severemalaria.org/sites/default/files/content/document/Digitization%20%20Directorate%20(Rwanda)%20.pdf)
