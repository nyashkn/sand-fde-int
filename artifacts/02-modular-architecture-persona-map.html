Module inventory — what each one actually is

Health Atlas
= Situation Map tab, Mapbox

Facility dots coloured by status over a district map. The 3D catchment-analysis work is real but was shown on a projector in Rwanda, not in the shipped web UI.

confirmed on video
Health Outcome Tracker
= “Health Outcome Visibility” pillar

Indicator rollups facility → district → national. In the UI this is the Clinical sub-tab: disease occurrence, burden per health post, prevalence by age/gender.

confirmed on video
Health Insight Engine
= the AI layer nobody has seen

Marketing claims anomaly detection and outbreak alerting; Rwanda's NHIC is genuinely used for Ebola surveillance. But zero alerting UI appears in either video — everything shown is descriptive BI.

claimed, not observed
Analytics Template Toolkit
= Apache Superset + saved templates

Superset is named explicitly in Sand's own FDE job posting. Its built-in scheduled-report feature is the single fastest route to automating the Quarterly Bulletin.

named in Sand JD
HealthOS Data Models
= the DHIS2 → warehouse transform

Not a user-facing product. Org unit × data element × period, reshaped into conformed dims and facts. dbt/Airflow listed as preferred FDE skills, consistent with a medallion pipeline.

pattern inferred
Bluelake Admin
= the actual product name on screen

Not in the brief at all, but it is what the UI calls itself at bluelake.rhos.africa. Four tabs, three sub-tabs, one persistent filter bar. Everything above renders through it.

confirmed on video

What this diagram changes about the build

The bulletin does not need a new product — it needs a mart and a Superset template. The hot path touches four things Sand already runs: a DHIS2 pull, a conformed mart, a saved dashboard, and Superset's scheduled-report feature. Nothing on that path is a greenfield build, which is what makes 6 weeks credible.

The Insight Engine is the honest gap. If the interview pushes on AI capability, the defensible answer is that the descriptive layer is real and shipped, the predictive layer is claimed but unevidenced — and that a bulletin built on the descriptive layer is the lower-risk first deliverable regardless.

175 paper-only facilities never reach any of these boxes. Every module above assumes a digital source. That constraint belongs in the scoping doc as an explicit out-of-scope line, not discovered in week 4.
