What you're feeling

Every element on these 4 screens is grounded in a specific frame from the source videos — not invented. Tags mark what's directly observed vs inferred.

  • Two-level nav, not one. Top nav picks the domain (Dashboard/Operations/Finance/Situation Map), sub-nav picks the lens (Operational/Financial/Clinical). observed
  • The filter bar never moves. Facility / date range / gender / age group sit above every screen — the same 4 controls slice every module. observed
  • Fridge temp/humidity charts prove real IoT cold-chain monitoring is live, not just a marketing claim — this is the kind of concrete capability worth citing directly in an interview. observed
  • Chart vocabulary repeats: line for trend, donut for totals, stacked/horizontal bar for breakdowns, dot map for geography — same 4 shapes everywhere. observed
  • Numbers on screen (28.03K visits, RF 14M revenue) are unverifiable — could be demo/synthetic data, not confirmed live production figures. flagged
  • "Reporting Completeness by Facility" bars are inferred from the assignment's Quarterly Bulletin requirement, not directly seen on screen — included here to test whether it fits the existing chart language. inferred
