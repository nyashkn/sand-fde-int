§4Withheld

Two panels a quarterly bulletin would normally carry are not printed. Both are withheld by automated guards rather than editorial judgement, and each records the measurement that decided it.

Withheld · trend against previous quarter

There is no genuine month-to-month signal in this data.

Shuffling each facility's months among themselves destroys any real ordering while preserving that facility's level. The shuffled null matches the observed autocorrelation, so all apparent movement is a stable per-facility baseline plus noise. A quarter-over-quarter arrow would be drawing noise.

Withheld · what drives mortality

Every candidate driver is confounded by facility tier.

Pooled across all 117 facilities, staff training correlates with mortality at -0.844, strong enough to look like a finding. Inside each tier it disappears. Both the training rate and the death count are downstream of where equipment and staff were placed, so the pooled figure measures tier and not training. Publishing it would point the Ministry at a lever that is not there.
